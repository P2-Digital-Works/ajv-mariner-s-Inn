GOOD TO KNOW

A few things to know.

We want guests to have the right information before making plans. Official policies will be published after the owner confirms them.

Are room prices final on the website?+

No rates are published in this concept. The staff must confirm pricing, inclusions, and any additional charges.

Does submitting an inquiry reserve a room?+

No. A reservation inquiry is not a confirmed booking. Staff must verify availability and reply with a confirmation.

What are the check-in and check-out times?+

Official times have not been verified. Contact the inn directly once the owner-approved contact information is published.

Can I pay for a room online?+

This MVP does not collect payments. Payment methods and policies must be confirmed by the inn.
