# AJV Mariner's Inn · Owner Pitch

## The proposition

A professional, mobile-first website that gives guests a clear way to explore the inn, send structured stay requests, and find its location. Inquiries still need staff confirmation. The concept is not a reservation engine or a property-management system.

## Short owner demonstration

1. **Discover:** Open the site on a mobile-sized browser, point out the rooms, gallery, and navigation.
2. **Choose:** Enter future dates and a party size. Explain that sample rooms demonstrate the experience, not actual inventory.
3. **Inquire:** Choose **Owner preview → Try guest booking flow**. This preloads a fictional example guest so no real personal information is needed.
4. **Receive:** Submit the sample request and show its clearly marked **pending staff confirmation** status.
5. **Manage:** Open **Owner preview → View demo inquiry inbox**. Show the sample request and CSV export; explain that real submissions would need configured Netlify Forms notifications or a backend.
6. **Launch plan:** Request actual room names, rates, photos, official phone/contact channels, property policies, privacy practices, and approval of the address/map pin.

## Proposal boundaries

- Site and source ownership must be agreed with the owner; recommend owner-controlled domain, hosting and business accounts.
- Quotation should separate one-time development from ongoing domain, maintenance, hosting and content updates.
- This concept has **no verified room inventory, rates, live availability, payment capability, confirmed bookings, or shared staff dashboard**.
- Production requires signed-off content and test submissions, privacy/retention procedures and notification verification.

## Suggested handover package

Responsive website and source code; domain and hosting setup; owner-approved content and photos; contact form integration; Search Console setup; owner training; written maintenance options; functional acceptance testing.
